Research Assistant 1.3.111

This update adds a numbers-first dashboard mode for users who want current ticker values and calculations before research workflow guidance.

- Adds a persistent Dashboard Mode setting with Numbers and Research modes.
- Makes Numbers mode the default dashboard experience.
- Keeps Numbers mode to Overview and Events & Sources tabs.
- Moves current quote values, valuation ratios, latest fiscal-year values, forward estimates, numeric metrics, technical chart, five-year financials, and analyst estimates into the Numbers overview.
- Keeps Research mode available with the Research Command Center, 10/10 Checkpoint, coach, guided session, memo, thesis notes, confidence panels, and full workflow tabs.
- Updates PDF export so reports begin with a full numeric workbook and can span multiple pages.

Research Assistant remains an educational research utility. It does not provide brokerage integration, trade execution, portfolio custody, or personalized investment advice.
