Research Assistant 1.7.131

- The Stock Price Chart now offers LTM, YTD, 2-year, 3-year, 5-year, and 10-year ranges using real loaded daily history.
- A standard 14-day Relative Strength Index panel adds a teal momentum line, latest value, neutral shading, and traditional 30 oversold and 70 overbought guides.
- Long-range charts render substantially faster while calculations and range summaries continue to use the full loaded history.
- Switching companies from Recent Symbols or Watchlist now shows available cached research immediately while fresh data loads, and a slower older request can no longer replace the newest selection.
- Independent market-data requests now load in parallel while preserving the existing request limiter and cache behavior.
- The interactive Numbers Only Mode tutorial includes 23 lessons, practice activities, progress tracking, and direct links into the relevant app workspaces.
- A brand-new tutorial now begins with Getting Started. Continue appears only after a valid saved lesson position or completed lesson exists.
